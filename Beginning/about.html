ABOUT AL-AMIN

Building Minds. Shaping Character.

A school where academic excellence, moral character and Islamic values come together.

OUR STORY

Growing With Purpose

Established in 1999, Al-Amin Group of Schools has grown with a commitment to providing quality education that develops the mind, character and spirit of every child.

As a Muslim educational institution, the school brings together Western and Islamic education. Alongside their academic subjects, students are exposed to Arabic studies, Islamic Religious Studies and Salah as part of their educational experience.

OUR PURPOSE

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

  • To provide affordable, quality education from pre-primary through secondary level.
  • To help students discover and develop their potential through proper guidance, values and experience.
  • To nurture students socially, morally, academically and spiritually.
  • To equip young people with the knowledege, character and practical skills needed to face the future with confidence.

Our Vision

To become a citadel of academic excellence where potentials are built and developed, and to rank among the very best private institutions in Nigeria.
